## Recommendation

**Bucket: `in-work`.** Leave the folder where it is.

Why: the daemon already runs class-aware recency on the default recall path, projects conflicts from the controlled-write hook, writes stale-ref verdicts from maintenance, and exposes config, history, and CLI list/resolve. That is real in-work code, not a backlog sketch and not withdrawn work, so `archive` and `backlog` are the wrong buckets. It is not `completed`: 16 acceptance criteria are absent in source (dashboard memories health and stale-ref surfacing, confirmed-useful reinforcement, confidence-exponent ranking, supersede exclusion on recall, operator reversal, verification-freshness scheduling). Three more need a live measurement that is not in the tree.

Counts: 65 acceptance criteria. **MET 46. UNMET 16. UNVERIFIABLE 3.**

## Where the behavior lives

- Capture (`src/daemon/runtime/capture/capture-handler.ts`) commits the turn. It does not detect conflicts, decay recency, or record reinforcement.
- Conflict detection runs after a controlled write commits, off the write critical section: `src/daemon/runtime/pipeline/controlled-writes.ts:534` `runConflictHook`, built at `src/daemon/runtime/assemble.ts:2914`.
- Recency, staleness demotion, ACT-R, calibration stamping, and the conflict gate run inside `recallMemories` (`src/daemon/runtime/memories/recall.ts:2885-2966`).
- `recallFast` (`fast: true` only) keeps Stage-1 recency and drops staleness, ACT-R, the conflict gate, calibration, and access recording (`recall.ts:3094-3095`, `recall.ts:3277-3284`). The default engine is `recallMemories` (`api.ts:806`).
- Maintenance ticks re-verify stale refs, compact the access log, and refit calibration (`assemble.ts` `armReverifyTick` at `3999`).

July 2026 QA (`qa/2026-07-06-prd-058-lifecycle-wiring-qa.md`) marked 54 of 57 ACs verified and deferred only AC-55d.2.1, AC-55d.2.2, and AC-55d.2.4. This pass keeps those three UNMET and overturns thirteen more. QA text is not evidence.

## Fast-path caveat (not a separate AC)

`POST /api/memories/recall` with `fast: true` uses `recallFast`. It applies Stage-1 `freshnessScore` and skips the conflict gate, staleness factor, ACT-R swap, calibration stamp, and access log (`recall.ts:3094-3095`, `:3277-3284`). Per-turn callers that opt into the fast lane do not get IDX-2 suppression or 058e activation. The default handler is still `recallMemories` (`api.ts:806`).
